# Heat illness SMS triage safety notes

This feature is decision support, not diagnosis, emergency monitoring, or a substitute for calling local emergency services. Red-flag rules must take precedence over AI. No AI response may downgrade a deterministic emergency result. Human medical review and native Bangla review are required before production use.

For suspected heatstroke, advise immediate emergency medical help and rapid cooling while help is coming: move to shade/cool area and use cold wet cloths/towels, fans, or other safe cooling. A confused or unconscious person must not be given food or drink. SMS wording must remain short and point to Bangladesh's configurable emergency number (default 999).

Source: WHO, [What to do if you see signs of heat stroke (16 May 2024)](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/environment-climate-change-and-health/signs-of-heat-stroke.pdf). WHO advises immediate emergency help, aggressive cooling, moving to shade/air conditioning, applying cold wet cloths, and monitoring until help arrives. The first aid copy in this application requires medical review before use.
